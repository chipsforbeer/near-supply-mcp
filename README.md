# Supplier Inventory MCP

An AI-assisted workspace for purchasing, inventory, and supplier coordination.
It helps teams keep business records organized and move from an identified need
to an approved order and recorded delivery.

## Capabilities

- Review stock, material requirements, and replenishment needs.
- Organize suppliers, contacts, quotes, and supporting documents.
- Compare offers against purchasing requirements.
- Prepare purchase orders for human approval.
- Record deliveries, quality decisions, and inventory changes.
- Maintain company knowledge and a history of decisions.
- Connect approved orders to the optional [NEAR Payment MCP](payment-mcp/README.md).

## Typical workflow

1. Identify what needs replenishing.
2. Review suppliers and compare offers.
3. Prepare an order and obtain approval.
4. Arrange an authorized payment when needed.
5. Record the delivery and review the outcome.

## Access and accountability

People work within their company's records and assigned permissions. Purchase
approvals and supplier payment details require an authorized approver. Recorded
changes include their reason so decisions can be reviewed.

Email and payment capabilities require separately configured integrations.
Credentials should remain private and should never be pasted into an AI chat.
